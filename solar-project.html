01 / Interactive sample

Try the sample calculator

This demonstration has two calculators to help potential customers understand possible solar savings and help the business estimate the cost of site surveys that do not convert.

☀

Solar Savings Calculator

Potential customers can estimate possible savings based on their monthly electric bill. This helps them understand the potential value of switching to solar.

▤

Site Survey Cost Calculator

Estimate the cost of site surveys that do not turn into sales. Adjust inquiries, site surveys, and closing rate to see what unconverted visits could be costing the business.

Why this matters: Not every inquiry is ready to buy. A qualification process helps prioritize prospects, reduce wasted site visits, and make follow-up more effective.
